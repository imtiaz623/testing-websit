About us

Groceries, the way your neighborhood grocer used to do it

FreshDrop started with a simple idea: buying groceries should feel as good as cooking with them. We work directly with local growers, bakers, and suppliers so every order arrives fresh, and every delivery is easy to track from the moment you place it.

We're a small, hands-on team focused on getting the essentials right — fast delivery, honest sourcing, and real people you can reach when you need help. As we grow, that focus doesn't change.
